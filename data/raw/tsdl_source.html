WORLD CUP 2026

The 2026 FIFA World Cup runs from 11th June to 19th July 2026. Hosted across 16 cities in three nations, USA, Canada, and Mexico. For the first time in history, 48 teams will compete across 104 matches in the expanded tournament, making it the biggest World Cup ever. This page brings you everything you need to follow the action, including standings, fixtures and results, team statistics such as corners, cards, xG, shots and more, and individual player stats from throughout the tournament. Whether you’re tracking your nation’s progress or scouting the Golden Boot race, you’ll find it all here.
